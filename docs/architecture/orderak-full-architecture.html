Market scope and data authority

Orderak launches commercially in Egypt, expands through MENA, and then expands globally. Egypt is a launch configuration, not a product or tenant boundary. D1 is authoritative for identity, onboarding, the versioned global business taxonomy, and the selected city from the pinned country-scoped static catalogue. Shared architecture must separate country, currency, timezone, locale, payment capabilities, and market readiness; current EGP/piaster and Egypt-specific payout fields require migration before a second market is activated.

Android is the only implemented seller client. Current portability work adds pre-release v1-only Seller API routing, separately versioned Admin/integration surfaces, an OpenAPI contract boundary, a platform-neutral client-context boundary, behavior-based safety contracts, and explicit sync/conflict rules; it does not add an iOS or seller Web/PWA component.

# Phase 4 public catalog content controls

## Baseline rules

Sellers may publish only lawful goods/services and content they are entitled to
use. The launch catalog must prohibit at minimum illegal goods/services,
financial or payment schemes outside the approved model, weapons and dangerous
items, controlled substances, counterfeit or infringing goods, deceptive claims,
adult/sexual services, exploitation, hate or harassment, malware/fraud, privacy
violations, and content involving children in an unsafe or unlawful manner.

Counsel must tailor the final categories to Egyptian law, competent-authority
requirements, merchant segment, and Google Play rules before pilot.

## Required workflow

1. A buyer, seller, staff member, authority, or automated control submits a report
   with URL/item, category, description, time, contact option, and evidence.
2. Support acknowledges and triages severity. Imminent harm, security, child
   safety, or valid authority requests use the emergency escalation path.
3. Authorized trust/support staff preserve minimum evidence, restrict access,
   and may temporarily hide content while review is pending.
4. A reviewer records the rule, facts, decision, affected content/store, action,
   notification basis, and retention/legal-hold decision.
5. Seller receives a reason and appeal route unless prohibited by law, safety,
   investigation integrity, or authority instruction.
6. A different authorized reviewer decides the appeal where practicable.
7. Repeated or severe violations trigger graduated controls up to store
   suspension/termination and authority escalation after counsel review.
8. Metrics track volume, severity, response/decision time, reinstatement,
   repeat offenders, evidence access, and unresolved high-risk cases.

## Acceptance criteria

- Public catalog and support resources expose a usable report route in Arabic
  and English before pilot.
- Cases have immutable IDs, timestamps, reporter/contact handling, content/store
  reference, category, severity, owner, status, evidence, action, appeal, and audit.
- Only authorized roles can hide, restore, suspend, export, or view sensitive evidence.
- Emergency actions are reversible when safe and reviewed within one business day.
- No content-control log includes unnecessary buyer or reporter personal data.
- Test evidence covers report, duplicate report, urgent escalation, takedown,
  seller notice, appeal, reinstatement, repeat offender, unauthorized action,
  and legal-hold/deletion interaction.

The complete case-management implementation is a G6/pilot blocker. This document
freezes requirements; it does not claim that the current admin panel already
implements every workflow step.

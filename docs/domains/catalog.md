# Catalog domain

Products, categories, their translations, and the public pages a buyer actually
sees.

Placing an order from those pages is the [orders domain](./orders.md). The
store that owns the catalog is the [stores domain](./stores.md).

## Runtime state

| Flag | Production | Staging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BUSINESS_TAXONOMY_ENABLED` | `true` | `true` |
| `STATIC_CITY_CATALOG_ENABLED` | `true` | `true` |

Both are on everywhere. Nothing in this domain is dark.

## Tables

`products`, `categories`, `product_translations`, `items`,
`business_categories`, `business_subcategories`, `business_taxonomy_versions`,
and — in a separate database — `geo_cities` and `geo_city_names`.

Money columns are `price_minor` plus an explicit `currency`, renamed and added
by migration `044_money_minor_units_with_currency.sql`. **Production is still on
the pre-044 names** until its next deploy — see
[schema skew](../data/database.md#staging-and-production-are-on-different-schemas-right-now). For the reasoning, see
[ADR-009](../decisions/adr-009-minor-units-with-explicit-currency.md).

Ownership is `store_id` on `products` and `categories`. Note that this is the
same value as `seller_id` elsewhere in the schema —
[identity](./identity.md#one-table-two-meanings) explains why two names exist.

## Public resource codes

Products and categories carry short public codes rather than exposing UUIDs:
`p-XXXXXX` for a product, `c-XXXXXX` for a category, generated by
`newResourceCode` from the same ambiguity-free alphabet as store codes. The
letters `c`, `p` and `s` are reserved slugs precisely so these prefixes cannot
collide with a store name — see [stores](./stores.md#slugs).

## Translations

`product_translations` holds per-locale product text, refreshed by
`refreshProductTranslations(env, storeId)` in
`services/backend/src/domains/catalog/product-translations.ts`.

The separation matters: **price, currency and stock are business data; name and
description are presentation data.** A locale is added by inserting
translations, never by adding a `name_ar` column. The rules that govern which
locales exist and how fallback behaves are the
[localization architecture](../architecture/localization-architecture.md) and
the [localization invariants](../contracts/localization-invariants.md), both of
which are authoritative over this page on that subject.

## Public pages

`services/backend/src/domains/catalog/catalog.ts` renders the buyer-facing
surface directly from the public Worker — there is no separate storefront
application:

- `renderStorePage(env, store, lang)`
- `renderCategoryPage(env, store, category, lang)`
- `renderProductPage(env, store, product, lang)`

Each takes an explicit locale. Pages are reached through the store's
`public_identifier` at `orderak.app/{identifier}`, resolved by
`findStoreByIdentifier` — see [stores](./stores.md#resolution).

## Business taxonomy

`business-taxonomy.ts` serves the versioned category tree a seller picks from
when describing their business — `business_categories`,
`business_subcategories`, and `business_taxonomy_versions`.

It is **versioned on purpose**: the taxonomy changes over time, and a store that
chose a category last year must keep meaning what it meant then. Reclassifying
stores silently by editing the tree in place is the failure this design
prevents.

## Geography

City data lives in `orderak_geo`, a **separate D1 database** from
`orderak_db` — `geo_cities` and `geo_city_names`, served by
`domains/catalog/geo.ts`.

Two properties are deliberate:

1. **It is isolated.** A pinned, licensed ODbL city catalogue has different
   provenance, update cadence and licence obligations from business data. Mixing
   them into one database would tangle backup, restore and attribution.
2. **It reads from replicas.** `geo.ts` calls
   `env.orderak_geo.withSession("first-unconstrained")`, which permits a
   read from any replica without a consistency constraint. City names do not
   change while a user is typing, so the latency win is free.

> **Read replication is not configured in `wrangler.jsonc`.** It is not a
> binding field — wrangler ignores it and warns. It must be enabled per database
> from the Cloudflare dashboard or REST API, and the `withSession` calls above
> only take effect once it is. See the comment in
> `services/backend/wrangler.jsonc`.

The verified phone country selects the catalogue subset; see
[stores](./stores.md#country).

## Boundaries

- **Order placement and lifecycle** are the [orders domain](./orders.md).
- **Store naming and resolution** are the [stores domain](./stores.md).
- **Product-count and category-count limits** are set by
  [entitlements](./entitlements.md) — `max_products` and `max_categories` — and
  enforced at the write path here.
- **Theme, colours and the design system** that style these pages are the
  design-system domain, not yet documented.
- **Which locales exist** is the localization architecture, not this page.

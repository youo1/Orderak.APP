# Onboarding taxonomy audit — 28 July 2026

> **Status:** Historical evidence. This page summarizes the durable conclusions
> of the AI-assisted workbook that was removed during the repository
> reorganization. It is not an authoritative runtime catalogue.

## Preserved conclusions

- The source contained 4,274 Google Business Profile categories.
- 68 source categories were publishable without consolidation, 1,224 were
  consolidated into broader Orderak categories, and 2,982 were excluded from
  the first release because they were low-confidence, unsuitable, sensitive,
  or regulated.
- The resulting version-1 published catalogue contained 113 active
  subcategories with Arabic, English, and French labels.
- The catalogue was designed without country or city dependencies.
- The audit did not change store URL identity or the existing eight-character
  store-code rules.

## Current authority

The workbook, rendered images, and raw audit rows were removed from the active
repository after this summary was recorded. Current behavior is defined by the
business-taxonomy migrations, backend implementation, API contract, and tests.
When they conflict with this historical summary, the current contract and
implementation win.

Privacy, Compliance, and Records Implementation Plan
1. Objective
Translate Egyptian privacy, cybercrime, consumer-protection, telecommunications, tax, payment, and fraud requirements into documented product and technical controls before the platform's infrastructure is finalized.
The principal legal baseline is Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and its Executive Regulations issued by Ministerial Decision No. 816 of 2025. The regulations address controller and processor licensing, secure processing records, Data Protection Officer registration, sensitive and children's data, direct marketing, security incidents, and cross-border transfers.
This plan is an implementation framework, not a substitute for a formal opinion from qualified Egyptian counsel.
